# The plan, one page (the 90 Day Plan: beat 2, and the block `improve` appends at beat 5)

`squad/90-day-plan.md`. One page, seen not read. This order. Nothing else.

Three strings are read back by name, so they are written exactly: the header line (the resume
check reads its `confirmed` stamp), the table's 5 columns (`improve` reads the Decide column), and
the `## SECOND 90 · <date>` heading (`improve` appends under it and never edits above it). It is a
plan, never a promise, and nothing in it predicts a result. No fenced block anywhere in the file:
the same text is rendered into a Google Doc, and a black box does not read there.

## The header line (the first line of the file, 4 fields)

`LANE <lane> · hours a day <N> · week 1 <Monday's date> · confirmed <date>`

Week 1 starts the Monday after the day the plan is written (today, when today is a Monday).
`confirmed <date>` is stamped at the yes and nowhere else; until then the slot reads
`confirmed (not yet)`.

## `THE FLOOR`

One line, stated once, never lowered, and the base's daily ten is where it comes from:

`10 by hand a day, Monday to Friday · 30 minutes · 50 a week · never lowered.`

Then where the names come from, one line, so a founder with no list is never sent to find 10
strangers at 8 in the morning. In this order: your sheet's live rows (`N rows pasted <date>`
when the founder pasted the Warm tab, `files only` when not), your client folders and who they
know, whoever answered a post, O1's list once it exists. When the names run short, the base's
shapes: a partnership, a meeting, an insight worth sending, a fund, a reply, a referral. Never
"the deck attached": the deck goes to its one buyer in the sales hour.

## `THE LANE` (the lane's weekly count, as a small table)

The arithmetic shown, so the founder can redo it when the hours change. The floor's 30 minutes
come off first. The by-hand count runs on the 4 Sell days, Wednesday to Saturday. Ad spend is the
one thing that runs all 7, so $100 a day is $700 a week, and the table says so. Three rows, the
third one the lane's own; the lane name is the line above the table.

OUTREACH, worked at 2 hours a day:

| The arithmetic | |
|---|---|
| minutes a day for the lane | 2 h x 60 - 30 = 90 |
| by hand a day | 90 / 3 = 30 |
| a week, Wed to Sat | 30 x 4 = 120, off O1's list |

Under it, one line: `The sequence's count is yours to report once O2 is live and warmed.`

CONTENT, worked at 2 hours a day:

| The arithmetic | |
|---|---|
| minutes a day for the lane | 2 h x 60 - 30 = 90 |
| Build, Mon and Tue | 180 minutes: 1 recording, cut and packaged |
| Sell, Wed to Sat | 1 piece published on Wed; 360 minutes on the floor and the replies |

ADS, worked at 2 hours a day:

| The arithmetic | |
|---|---|
| spend | $100 a day flat, all 7 days: $700 a week, behind a1's gate |
| Build, Mon and Tue | 180 minutes on the creative, one concept |
| Sell, Wed to Sat | 90 minutes a day on the read and the replies |

OUTREACH rounds down to a 5 and caps at 50 a day above the floor, and the line under the table
says the cap out loud when it bites: `50 by hand a day, the cap; the inbox is the limit, not the
hours`. Under 1 hour the table is replaced by one line: `the floor is the plan this 90; the lane
opens the week the hours reach 1`.

## `THE BLOCK`

One fixed hour, Monday to Saturday: `9:00 to 11:00, Monday to Saturday` for 2 hours a day,
proposed at 9:00 and confirmed or changed at the yes. Monday and Tuesday the block builds;
Wednesday to Saturday it sells; Sunday has no block, only the read. Outreach at 9 beats outreach
sometime.

## `THE GATES` (the phase gates, no dates)

2 lines, and no date on either, because a gate is passed by evidence and not by the calendar:

`Money received ends phase one.`
`Three clients from one channel ends phase two.`

## The miss clause (one line above the table)

`One zero day is a row, not a quit. Never two in a row.`

## The table (13 rows, 5 columns)

`| Week | Dates | Build (Mon, Tue) | Sell (Wed to Sat) | Decide (Sun) |`

- **Week**: 1 to 13. An appended block numbers its rows 1 to 13 again, under its own block
  heading; the block heading is what tells them apart.
- **Dates**: Monday to Sunday, `Sep 7 to 13`, counted from the header's week 1 date.
- **Build (Mon, Tue)**: the lane's build for that week, the same shape every week, then the
  milestone marker on the weeks that carry one. Everything else in the cell is the build alone;
  a week invented tonight is a promise this file does not make.

  | Lane | The build, every week |
  |---|---|
  | OUTREACH | `the list (o1) · the deck for one name (g7)`. Week 1 is `the list (o1)` alone |
  | CONTENT | `1 recording, packaged off the winner in your topic (c1, c2)` |
  | ADS | `the creative, one concept` |

  The markers, one line each, after the build:

  | Lane | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 7 | Week 13 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | OUTREACH | the first list | the first deck, the first follow-ups | the first batch read (o3) | halfway read | /90days improve |
  | CONTENT | the first recording, the first piece | the first Sunday read (c6) | | halfway read | /90days improve |
  | ADS | blank until the ads track names them | | | halfway read | /90days improve |

- **Sell (Wed to Sat)**: the floor first, with its own days attached, then the lane's weekly count
  off THE LANE, the same every week: OUTREACH `50 by hand (10 a day, Mon to Fri) · 120 off the
  list` (at 2 hours), CONTENT `50 by hand (10 a day, Mon to Fri) · 1 piece published`, ADS `50 by
  hand (10 a day, Mon to Fri) · $700 spend, all 7 days`. Under 1 hour the cell reads `50 by hand
  (10 a day, Mon to Fri)`.
- **Decide (Sun)**: blank when the plan is written. Every Sunday the founder types the one change
  for next week into that week's cell in this file on disk, off `/bip sunday` or the track's read
  (o3, c6 or a4). The Google Doc is a copy to read, not the copy that is read back. This skill
  never writes the cell; `improve` reads all 13 at week 13.

## Never in the file

A sequence count (O2 owns the sequence), a cut into pieces, a hot seat time, a scorecard or a
pace line, a shrink rule, a day-100 date, a morning, a supply list, a projected result, a fenced
block.

## The block `improve` appends (beat 5, one per run)

Under `## SECOND 90 · <today's date>` (THIRD, FOURTH on later runs), in this order, and not one
line above the heading is touched:

1. the header line again, with the new hours and the lane, `confirmed (not yet)` until the yes
2. `KEEP`, `KILL`, `CHANGE`, one line each, off beat 5
3. `THE LANE`, the table recomputed off the new hours
4. `THE BLOCK`
5. the miss clause and the 13-row table, its weeks numbered 1 to 13 again and dated on from the
   week after the last row above. Build carries the CHANGE line as week 1's marker, `halfway
   read` in week 7 and `/90days improve` in week 13, and nothing else. Decide is blank again.

## The Google Doc (beat 3, when a Drive connector answers)

The same file rendered as HTML and handed to `create_file` as `text/html`: the title as `<h1>`,
the header line as a `<p>`, each caps label as an `<h2>` with its lines under it, the arithmetic
and the 13 weeks as `<table>` elements with a header row, the miss clause as a `<p>`. Nothing in
the Doc that is not in the file, and nothing in the file that is not in the Doc.

The first Doc is titled "90-day plan · <founder name>". A Doc's body cannot be rewritten, so
`improve` makes a new one at beat 6, titled "90-day plan · <founder name> · <today's date>", and
the previous one is renamed "90-day plan · <founder name> (superseded)" where the connector can
rename it. The founder types the Sunday change into `squad/90-day-plan.md` on disk; the Doc is a
copy to read, and it is not read back.

## Formatting

Plain markdown. The weeks go in the table, the arithmetic in its table, everything else in short
lines. No em dashes. No fenced block. No prediction, no projected revenue, no promised result
anywhere in the file.
