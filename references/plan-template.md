# The plan, one page (the 4-Week Plan: beat 2, the 2 cells beat 4 writes, and the block `renew` appends at beat 5)

`squad/90-day-plan.md`. One page per plan, seen not read. This order. Nothing else.

It is written to the document law: a heading per section, one-line paragraphs, a table wherever
there are 3 or more columns of facts, the line to say out loud in bold, no paragraph over 40
words. The same text is rendered into a Google Doc with real headings and tables, so there is no
fenced block anywhere in the file. It is a plan, never a promise, and nothing in it predicts a
result.

Three strings are read back by name, so they are written exactly: the header line (the resume
check reads its `confirmed` stamp and its renew date), the table's 4 columns (beat 4 writes
Measure and Improve, `renew` reads them), and the `## PLAN N of 3 · <date>` heading (`renew`
appends under it and never edits above it).

## The heading and the header line (the first 2 lines of every block)

Every block opens the same way, plan 1 included, so `renew` appends under a heading rather than
beside a headingless block and "the newest block" is always findable:

`## PLAN 1 of 3 · <today's date>`

`PLAN 1 of 3 · LANE <lane> · hours a day <N> · week 1 <Monday's date> · renew <Monday's date> · confirmed <date>`

Week 1 starts the Monday after the day the plan is written (today, when today is a Monday). The
renew date is the Monday after week 4; the renewal itself is written on week 4's Sunday.
`confirmed <date>` is stamped at the yes and nowhere else; until then the slot reads
`confirmed (not yet)`.

## `## The cycle`

Four lines, the first in bold, the same in every plan:

**Run what already wins. Measure the results. Improve what won.**

`Run, Monday to Saturday: the making usually Monday and Tuesday, the send or publish from Wednesday.`
`Measure, Sunday: 2 numbers, off things that have finished.`
`Improve, Sunday: one line, a named change or holding until <number>, week N. It is Monday's brief.`

## `## The plan` (the header, as a 2-column table)

| The plan | |
|---|---|
| Lane | OUTREACH, CONTENT or ADS, the founder's word |
| Hours a day for the system | N |
| The block | `9:00 to 11:00, Monday to Saturday` for 2 hours, proposed at 9:00 and confirmed or changed at the yes. Sunday has no block |
| The hour | `1 hour a day on people, by hand. A habit, not a count.` The same line in every plan |
| The send or publish day | OUTREACH `Wednesday: the week's list enters` (the count, when it is written out, is the list's). CONTENT `Wednesday: the video is published`. ADS `Wednesday: the new ad set goes live on your go` |
| Plan | 1 of 3 |
| Renew | the Monday after week 4; the renewal is written on week 4's Sunday |
| The gates | `Money received ends phase one. 3 clients from one channel ends phase two.` No date on either: a gate is passed by evidence, not by the calendar |
| The read lag | `Sunday reads only what has finished: a batch past its day-9 touch, a video past 7 days, an ad set past 7 days and about 50 events. The rest is held, never guessed.` |

## `## The arithmetic` (the lane's count, as a small table)

The arithmetic shown, so the founder can redo it when the hours change. The hours are the
system's, whole. Three rows, the lane's own; the lane name is the line above the table.

OUTREACH, worked at 2 hours a day:

| The arithmetic | |
|---|---|
| minutes a day for the system | 2 x 60 = 120 |
| by hand a day | 120 / 3 = 40 |
| a week, Wednesday to Saturday | 40 x 4 = 160: the 50 names' 3 touches, by your hand until the routine sends (o4) |

OUTREACH rounds down to a 5 and caps at 50 a day, and the line under the table says the cap out
loud when it bites: `50 by hand a day, the cap; the inbox is the limit, not the hours`. When a
day x 4 is under 150, the third row ends `: the week's list is 25 rows`.

CONTENT, worked at 2 hours a day:

| The arithmetic | |
|---|---|
| minutes a day for the system | 2 x 60 = 120 |
| the making, Monday and Tuesday | 240 minutes: the scrape, the package, the script, and Tuesday's block is the record |
| 1 recording a week | published Wednesday, the same slot every week; Wednesday to Saturday the minutes go on the cut, the listing and the replies |

ADS, worked at 2 hours a day and a $100 daily number:

| The arithmetic | |
|---|---|
| spend | the money card's daily number (a2), all 7 days: worked here at $100 a day, $700 a week, behind a1's gate. No card yet, the `ads gate` row's monthly / 30 is the daily and the working is shown; neither on disk, this row reads `your daily number (a1, a2)` and no figure is printed |
| the making, Monday and Tuesday | 240 minutes on one concept, cut 3 ways |
| from Wednesday | 120 minutes a day on the read and the leads; the ad set runs untouched |

## `## The weeks` (the miss clause, then the table)

One line above the table: `A missed week is written, never made up.`

Then the table, 4 rows, 4 columns:

`| Week | Run | Measure | Improve |`

- **Week**: 1 to 4, with the dates, `1 · Sep 7 to 13`, counted from the header's week 1 date. An
  appended block numbers its rows 1 to 4 again, under its own heading; the heading is what tells
  them apart.
- **Run**: written when the plan is written. The asset made that week AND the send or publish that
  puts it in front of strangers, in one cell, a middle dot between them. No cell names a day
  except the send or publish trigger. The cells per lane, at 2 hours a day:

  | Week | OUTREACH, warm domain, worked at 2 hours a day |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | The 50, one trade in one town, one broken thing per row, and the 3 touches (o1, o2) · the 50 enter Wednesday; touch 1 and the day-4 touch go out by your hand, about 100 |
  | 2 | The next 50, the second town, the same 3 touches · 50 enter Wednesday; batch 1's day-9 touch lands; about 150 out |
  | 3 | 50 in a second trade, the first town: one variable changed · 50 enter Wednesday; about 150 out |
  | 4 | The deck for the one name worth an hour (g7); no new list · no new batch; batch 3's day-4 and day-9 touches land, about 100 out |

  Under the warm-domain table, the cells are resized to the hours, never copied: the list is 50
  rows, or 25 when `by hand a day x 4` is under 150, and the out-counts follow it (week 1 the list
  x 2, weeks 2 and 3 the list x 3, week 4 the list x 2).

  | Week | OUTREACH, new domain (said at the gate) |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | 25 rows, one trade in one town, and the 3 touches (o1, o2) · touch 1 goes out at 10 a day from Wednesday; the day-4 and day-9 clocks run per name |
  | 2 | 25 rows, the second town · touch 1 at 15 a day |
  | 3 | The 50, a second trade, the first town · touch 1 at 20 a day |
  | 4 | The 50, the second town · touch 1 at 25 a day |

  Under the new-domain table, one line: `This plan reads deliverability only: bounces and
  placement. The first reply read is plan 2's.`

  | Week | CONTENT |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | 1 video: the winner in your topic copied, packaged, scripted, cut (c1 to c4) · published Wednesday; the post every night (g3) |
  | 2 | 1 video, the same slot (c1 to c4) · published Wednesday; the post every night |
  | 3 | 1 video, the same slot · published Wednesday; the post every night |
  | 4 | 1 video, the same slot · published Wednesday; the post every night |

  | Week | ADS |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | Concept 1, cut 3 ways, built paused (a2, a3) · ad set 1 live Wednesday on your go, at your daily number, untouched once it starts |
  | 2 | Concept 2, cut 3 ways · ad set 2 opens beside it Wednesday; ad set 1 untouched; dead cuts killed on the 2x rule |
  | 3 | Concept 3, cut 3 ways · ad set 3 opens Wednesday; the weakest finished ad set turned off, never edited |
  | 4 | Concept 4, cut 3 ways · ad set 4 opens Wednesday; 2 or 3 ad sets live at any time |

- **Measure**: blank when the plan is written. Sunday's 2 numbers, by the founder's line ("put this
  in week N"), off finished things only: OUTREACH `delivered · replies` (o3), CONTENT `7-day views
  · asks` (c6), ADS `cost per lead · leads` (a4). A number that has not finished reads `held until
  <when>`. Week 1's Sunday reads sent and delivered counts and little else.
- **Improve**: blank when the plan is written. Sunday's one line, by the founder's line, in
  exactly 2 forms: one named change, or `holding until <number>, week N`. Most cells read holding.
  The change is Monday's brief, never an edit to what is running.

## Never in the file

A daily task column, a day count, a 13th row, a first-touch count for the hour, a sequence count
before the routine sends (o2 and o4 own the send), a scorecard or a pace line, a projected
result, a fenced block.

## The block `renew` appends (beat 5, one per run)

Under `## PLAN 2 of 3 · <today's date>` (PLAN 3 of 3 next; after plan 3 the count starts again at
plan 1 of 3 of the next 90, and the heading carries a running number too: `## PLAN 4 · plan 1 of
3 · <date>`), in this order, and not one line above the heading is touched:

1. the header line again, its plan number, the new hours and the lane, `confirmed (not yet)`
   until the yes
2. `## The cycle`, the same 4 lines
3. `## The plan`, the header table with the new hours, block and renew date
4. `## The arithmetic`, recomputed off the new hours
5. the miss clause and the 4-row table, its weeks numbered 1 to 4 again and dated on from the
   renew date. Week 1's Run carries the last written Improve line as its brief: a named change
   becomes what week 1 makes and puts out; a hold keeps the Run as the lane ran it. Measure and
   Improve are blank again.

## The Google Doc (beat 3, when a Drive connector answers)

The same file rendered as HTML and handed to `create_file` as `text/html`: the title as `<h1>`,
the header line as a `<p>`, each `##` as an `<h2>`, one `<p>` per line, the 3-word line in `<b>`,
the header table, the arithmetic and the 4 weeks as `<table>` elements with a header row, the
miss clause as a `<p>`. Nothing in the Doc that is not in the file, and nothing in the file that
is not in the Doc.

The first Doc is titled "4-week plan · <founder name>". A Doc's body cannot be rewritten, so
`renew` makes a new one at beat 5, titled "4-week plan · <founder name> · plan 2" (its plan
number), and the previous one is renamed "4-week plan · <founder name> · plan 1 (done)" where the
connector can rename it. The Sunday cells are written into `squad/90-day-plan.md` on disk; the
Doc is a copy to read, and it is not read back.

## Formatting

Plain markdown. The header, the arithmetic and the weeks go in their tables, everything else in
short lines. No em dashes. No fenced block. No prediction, no projected revenue, no promised
result anywhere in the file.
